Stac Pollaidh
at Sunrise

Stac Pollaidh has always stood out to me. It’s not the biggest hill in Assynt but the ridge looks dramatic from the road and it’s one of those peaks that makes you want to see what’s up there.

We set off in the dark, four of us and our two dogs pushing on steadily without stopping. By the time we reached half way the sun was rising above Cùl Mòr and we stopped to take it in, with clouds rolling into the valley we’d just climbed out of.

The summit was in whiteout, but we could see the cloud shifting so we waited. It only took a few minutes before it cleared, gradually at first and then suddenly opening out across Inverpolly with Suilven, Canisp and the hills beyond.

We went looking for the true summit but couldn’t find a safe way up. What we found instead was an incredible view over Loch Lurgainn with small patches of inversion sitting on the water below.

On the way down we spotted a couple of deer on the hillside. They moved ahead of us as we descended, keeping just far enough away to feel safe until eventually they peeled off and we lost sight of them.
Before you go
How long Allow 3 to 4 hours so you have time for photos.
In the area
Food
Ullapool is 25 minutes away and has The Seafood Shack, try the battered haddock wrap after a long walk.
