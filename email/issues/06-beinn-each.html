Issue 6

Wild camping
on Beinn Each

I’ve climbed Beinn Each a few times. It’s ideally placed between a few larger mountains and has wide views over the rest of the Trossachs. The walk starts through a small forest before opening into Glen Ample, following a rough track to the base of the mountain and then climbing steadily up the east side, passing small lochans on the way.

On this trip my wife, our dog and I decided to wild camp near the summit. It was a bright warm day and we walked in t-shirts with heavy bags, one carrying the tent and supplies, the other with sleeping bags and water. We found a flat(ish) patch of grass around six hundred metres up with open views towards Stob Binnein, Ben More and the rest of the Trossachs.

After setting up camp we climbed to the summit in the golden hour. The low sun created layers of hills, tinged everything with orange and, as it dropped further, reduced the landscape to just those two things; sun and shadow. Back at our tent we watched the last light disappear before quickly getting into our sleeping bags as the temperature dropped. Overnight it reached minus five but with two of us and a dog in the tent it stayed warm enough.

First thing, we packed up, picked up our frozen bags and headed back down to the car. By the time we reached the glen the sun was up and all we could think about was breakfast.
 
Before you go
Getting there Park at Ardchullarie More and follow the path from there. Details of the route are on Walkhighlands.
Pack Water can be heavy, but still bring more than you think you’ll need
Wrap up Layers are essential; temperatures can drop fast, even after warm days
Where to pitch It’s a steady climb with plenty of camping spots around six hundred metres if you want the same view
Sleep at home You don’t have to camp. Beinn Each is still a great daytime walk
 
In the area
Detour
Beinn Each can be climbed together with Ben Vorlich and Stùc a’ Chroin for a longer route, or even a two-day trip with a camp in between.
Food
The village of Strathyre is nearby and has a café serving breakfast and good food throughout the day.
