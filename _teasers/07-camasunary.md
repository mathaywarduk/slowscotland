We were staying with friends in Plockton and decided on a drive over to Skye for a walk to Camasunary. The bay can't be reached by road, only by foot or by boat, so we parked in a small layby and set off with the dogs.

The path starts slowly, across open moorland with little to see at first, but as it begins to rise the Cuillin start to appear in the distance. By the time we reached the top of the hill the view opened wide, with the mountains falling sharply to the sea, the cliffs catching the light, and small clouds drifting over the peaks.

We carried on to the shore as the sky began to change colour, from yellow to soft pinks. Down by the bothy we wandered along the bay, looked out towards Rum and shared a beer as the light faded.
