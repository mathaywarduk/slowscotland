The North Coast 500 is busy these days, but there is a section on the west coast that still feels a bit wild. The road that takes you from Lochinver through Drumbeg and back to the A894 near Kylesku is one of the most beautiful drives in Scotland.

We've stayed in Drumbeg before and driven this road a few times now. It never feels the same twice. It winds through the knock-and-loch landscape that makes Assynt so different from anywhere else, with a different view around every corner. One minute you're looking across a loch to Quinag, the next you're driving towards a small beach or passing through a village.

It's especially beautiful in the golden hour when the low light changes the colour of the rocks and the water. Beaches like Clashnessie and Achmelvich are great places to see that light.

On one drive, we saw an eagle perched on a rock beside the road. As we slowed down to gawp at it, it took off, flying straight ahead of us for a few seconds before turning away. We could see the texture in its feathers and the wrinkles in its talons. I don't think I'll ever forget that.
