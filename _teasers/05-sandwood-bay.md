A few years back, we'd planned to climb Suilven on our anniversary. The day before we wanted something easier but still worth the effort. Sandwood Bay felt like the right choice.

It's a couple of hours walk along a track that winds through open moorland. There isn't much to look at on the way, just heather, lochans and the occasional sheep, but as we got closer to the coast we could start to make out the sound of the sea and the dunes began to rise in the distance. Then, suddenly, we were there.

Sandwood Bay is one of the most beautiful beaches in Scotland. Wide pink sand, a sea stack at one end and a small loch sitting behind the dunes. We wandered along the shore for a while, ate lunch behind a ridge of sand and watched the waves break. On the way back the dog went swimming in the loch and we made our way back to the path, through the tall grass behind it.

An easy, fulfilling day.
