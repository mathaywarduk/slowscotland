During our three-week trip around Scotland we took the ferry over to Mull. It was September but the weather was perfect, the sun shimmering on the water as we crossed and dolphins, or maybe porpoises, swimming alongside the boat.

We had booked a campsite on the southern peninsula but had also heard about Uisken Bay, a small piece of land right on the beach where the local croft owner allows people to camp for a small fee. We weren't sure if it was true but soon enough we found the honesty box, made our payment and drove onto the grass beside the sand.

We parked up for the evening, just the three of us. There were a couple of other vans around but it still felt quiet and like we had the place to ourselves. The tide was out and the sea was pretty shallow so we took the dog for a run, paddle, and a swim where it got deep enough. Later on, we built a small fire on the beach and sat by it with a drink as the sun went down.
