We were staying at Kylesku Lodges, looking out each morning at Quinag, my favourite mountain. I love it because it’s so gnarly looking and different from every angle. We were itching to get up there. We always try to climb a mountain on our anniversary and had planned to take on all three peaks, so we wanted something easier for the day before.

We decided on the track to Glendhu Bothy. A friend had told me about it years ago and it had been on my list ever since. The route follows the northern shore of Loch Gleann Dubh with Quinag in view for most of the way. It was a calm sunny day with a few clouds drifting over the hills.

We stopped often, letting the dog play in the water and watching birds in the ferns. At one point we spotted a seal just offshore. My wife was convinced it was following us. Eventually the bothy appeared in the distance, just between the loch and the hills. It was empty when we arrived so we looked around, sat outside and had lunch before making our way back.

The next morning I woke up early to climb Quinag but Covid had other plans. We spent the next week in bed instead.
