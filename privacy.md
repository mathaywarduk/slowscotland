Slow Scotland is run by Mat Hayward. When you sign up, we collect your email address so we can send you the series.

We use [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/legal/privacy/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} to store your email address and send our emails. Mailchimp processes your data on our behalf.

When you buy an itinerary, the checkout is run by [Payhip](https://payhip.com/privacy){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which takes your email address, country and payment details to process the order and send you the download link. Card payments are handled by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. We never see your card details. Payhip shares your email address and order details with us so we can help if anything goes wrong with your order.

Buying an itinerary doesn't sign you up to the email series. We'll only email you about your order, unless you tick the box at checkout to get the occasional email about new itineraries and quieter places in Scotland.

We use [Plausible](https://plausible.io/data-policy){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} to count visits to this website, including clicks on the buy buttons and completed orders. It doesn't use cookies or collect any personal information.

After you sign up, you'll receive 12 emails over 12 weeks, one a week. After that, we'll send occasional emails. They'll always be about Scotland, travel and quieter places.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. If you have any questions about your data, or would like it deleted, email [hello@slowscotland.uk](mailto:hello@slowscotland.uk).
