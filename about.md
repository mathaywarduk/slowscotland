I’m Mat, a photographer who’s spent the past decade exploring the Highlands and islands, and I now live in Scotland. Over the years I’ve found places that feel quieter, more rewarding and sometimes overlooked.

Slow Scotland shares some of them. There’s a twelve-week email series, with a photograph and a short story from one of my trips each week, along with a few practical notes if you want to visit yourself. Sometimes that means a good walk, sometimes a beach or viewpoint, and sometimes somewhere to eat or stay nearby.

Itineraries are coming soon, built from the same trips, for when you want to plan your own.

If you want to know why it’s called Slow Scotland, I’ve written about [what slow travel means to me](/slow-travel/).
