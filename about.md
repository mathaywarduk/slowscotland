I'm Mat, a photographer who's spent the past decade exploring the Highlands and islands, and now live in Scotland. Over the years I've found places that feel quieter, more rewarding, and sometimes overlooked.

Slow Scotland is a 12-week series that shares these places with you. Each week I'll send a photograph and a short story from one of my trips, along with a few practical notes if you want to visit yourself. Sometimes that will mean a good walk, sometimes a beach or viewpoint, and sometimes a suggestion for food or somewhere to stay nearby.

The idea is simple: spend less time following the crowd and more time enjoying Scotland at its best.
