The Trossachs is where the Highlands begin, with plenty of big hills and even more lochs. Some of them are tourist traps, but others are very quiet, and those are the ones this itinerary is built around. It keeps you in one place, with hill walks at sunset and golden hour, a sauna and a loch swim, a day on the water and a long day out to Loch Fyne.
