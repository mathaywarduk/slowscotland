Skye has become one of the busiest places in Scotland, but you only have to move away from the well known spots for a while and it goes quiet again. This itinerary starts on the mainland around Glenelg, crosses to Skye on the old turntable ferry and stays on the quieter south of the island, with long walks to remote beaches, swims in waterfall pools and sunsets over the Cuillin.
