Islands are slower and quieter, with small secluded beaches, rock pools and wildlife all round you, and you’ll often have them to yourself. This campervan itinerary covers Mull, Eigg or both, with nights on the beach at Uisken Bay and Calgary Bay, a walk over the hill to Sanna Bay on Ardnamurchan, and a few days on Eigg, where there are no cars at all.
